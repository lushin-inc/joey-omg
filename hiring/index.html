OMG Sales Assessment

How to Use for Hiring

The process

  1. Post the ad and start getting applicants
  2. Send the assessment before you ever talk to anyone
  3. Decide who to interview based on the recommendation:
    • RecommendedInterview
    • Worthy of ConsiderationInterview only if you're light on applicants
    • Not RecommendedNo interview

Built around your role

  • About 25 questions set it up for your selling environment:
    • Who you sell to (consumers, in your case)
    • Your average sale size
    • How much resistance your salespeople face
  • Someone who sells quarter-million-dollar deals well may not be able to sell a $1,000 deal
  • Someone used to no-resistance selling may not be able to sell where there's resistance

What to expect

  • 20-30% of candidates come back Recommended
  • You do fewer interviews, and the ones you do are strong
  • Worthy of Consideration candidates are a couple notches away; the report shows what they're missing for your role
  • 92% of Recommended hires end up in the top half of the sales force within six months
  • If they don't, OMG gives you the license back to do it again for free

Cost

  • $4,000
  • Unlimited assessments for 12 months, or until you hire
  • Works the same whether you're hiring one person or ten
